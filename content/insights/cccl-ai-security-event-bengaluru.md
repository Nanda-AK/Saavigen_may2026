On a Sunday evening when most people were winding down their weekend, over 70 security professionals, engineers and AI enthusiasts chose instead to spend their time learning.

SaaviGenAI was proud to sponsor the Anthropic CCCL (Claude Community) AI Security event in Bengaluru. It was an evening that reaffirmed just how strong the appetite for AI Security knowledge has become in India's tech community.

The event was organized in support of [Vikram Pawar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vikrammpawar), Claude Community Ambassador and Founder CCCL.ai, with the support of Anthropic. It continued CCCL's mission of building a grounded, technically rigorous AI community in India. [View the event agenda](https://cccl-ai.github.io/meetups-live/blr6/agenda.html).

## Technical depth meets real-world application

The evening featured a strong lineup of speakers covering both the technical depth and real-world application of AI Security.

![Collage of speakers presenting at the Anthropic CCCL AI Security event](/images/insights/cccl/speaker-collage.webp)

- **[Dr. Chinmay](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chinmayth)**: Delivered a deep technical session on AI security concepts and emerging threat models.
- **[Lakshmi Narasimhan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lakshnarasimhan/)**: Shared insights on AI security, contributing to the technical depth of the evening.
- **[Aranya Bandhu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aranyabandhu/)**: Brought real-world context to the discussion, grounding the evening's themes in practical application.
- **[Geetansh Aditya](https://www.linkedin.com/in/geetansh-aditya/)**: Contributed further perspective on applying AI security concepts in practice.
- **[Rohit Madhavan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rohitmadhavan/)**: Added practitioner perspective on securing AI-powered applications in production environments.
- **[Nanda, Founder of SaaviGenAI](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nandakumar80/)**: Presented on Anthropic's Constitutional AI (CAI), exploring how alignment principles are shaping the next generation of safe AI systems.

## Community is core to our mission

At SaaviGenAI, we believe the shift toward agentic AI is expanding the security threat surface faster than most organizations are prepared for. Events like this one are core to our mission. Building SaaviAIDR is one part of that work. Actively contributing to a community that takes AI Security as seriously as AI adoption itself is the other.

We're committed to continuing to support and participate in initiatives like CCCL that bring together practitioners, researchers and builders to advance the state of AI Security knowledge in India.

## What we were founded to solve

Events like this one connect directly to the problem SaaviGenAI was founded to solve: making enterprise-grade AI security accessible, consistent and affordable.

**SaaviAIDR** is our AI Detection and Response platform. It is purpose-built to monitor the AI layer itself, including LLMs, autonomous agents, RAG pipelines and the broader AI toolchain. SaaviAIDR detects and responds to AI-native threats such as unauthorized tool or API calls, data exfiltration through AI agents, prompt injection and misuse of AI-connected systems.

SaaviAIDR reflects SaaviGenAI's core thesis. As organizations adopt agentic AI, the threat surface expands from *text safety* (chatbot-level guardrails) to *action security* (AI agents taking real-world actions). Community events like the CCCL AI Security session help us stay close to how these threats are changing. They also keep SaaviAIDR shaped around real, emerging risks rather than theoretical ones.

> This event is one of several community touchpoints SaaviGenAI plans to support in the coming months, as we continue building toward a future where AI security is proactive, accessible and built into the foundation of how organizations adopt AI rather than bolted on as an afterthought.
