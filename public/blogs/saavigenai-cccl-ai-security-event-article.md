## About SaaviSOC and SaaviAIDR

Events like this one connect directly to the problem SaaviGenAI was founded to solve: making enterprise-grade AI security accessible, consistent, and affordable.

### What is SaaviSOC?

**SaaviSOC** is a Managed SOC (Security Operations Center delivered as a service — SOC-as-a-Service) built by SaaviGenAI using open-source SIEM technology combined with an AI-driven detection and analysis layer. By replacing expensive proprietary SIEM licensing and large in-house analyst teams with an AI-augmented model, SaaviSOC significantly reduces both the capital expenditure (CAPEX) and operating expenditure (OPEX) typically required to run a SOC — while maintaining consistent, round-the-clock monitoring and threat detection. SaaviSOC is designed for organizations that need enterprise-grade security operations without enterprise-grade security budgets.

### What is SaaviAIDR?

**SaaviAIDR (AI Detection & Response)** is SaaviGenAI's flagship product within the SaaviSOC platform. Where traditional security tools like EDR (Endpoint Detection & Response), NDR (Network Detection & Response), and XDR/MDR are built to monitor infrastructure, endpoints, and networks, SaaviAIDR is purpose-built to monitor the AI layer itself — LLMs, autonomous agents, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines, and the broader AI toolchain. SaaviAIDR detects and responds to AI-native threats such as unauthorized tool or API calls, data exfiltration through AI agents, prompt injection, and misuse of AI-connected systems — threats that fall outside the visibility of traditional SOC tooling.

Together, SaaviSOC and SaaviAIDR reflect SaaviGenAI's core thesis: as organizations adopt agentic AI, the threat surface expands from *text safety* (chatbot-level guardrails) to *action security* (AI agents taking real-world actions). Community events like the CCCL AI Security session are part of how SaaviGenAI stays close to this evolving threat landscape — and how we continue shaping SaaviSOC and SaaviAIDR around real, emerging risks rather than theoretical ones.
